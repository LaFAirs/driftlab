# Getting help

* **Questions / ideas** – [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/LaFAirs/driftlab/discussions)
* **Bugs** – [open an issue](https://github.com/LaFAirs/driftlab/issues/new/choose) with the bug-report form (controller, OS, firmware, log from *Settings → Export logs*)
* **New controller** – use the *Add a controller* issue form
* **Hardware problems** – if DriftLab says your stick hardware is probably worn, contact the manufacturer's support (warranty) or consider a replacement/Hall-effect stick module. DriftLab cannot repair potentiometer wear.
* **Security** – see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
